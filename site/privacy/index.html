Privacy policy

LetsStudy is operated by Valls Solutions LLC. Contact us at admin@vallssolutions.com about privacy or data requests.

Age-based experiences

On first launch, you choose 9–12, 13–17 or 18 and older. We store the chosen age range on your device, not your birth date. The 9–17 experiences contain curated lessons and save answers only on the device. They do not offer an account, public community, online AI, microphone, file import or personal profile. LetsStudy is not intended for children under 9. The 18+ experience offers the features described below.

Information we handle

  • Local study data: lessons, imported text, questions, answers, tutor conversations, settings and progress are stored on your device. A profile photo remains local unless an adult separately chooses to share a small thumbnail on the community board.
  • Optional adult account: when you create an account, Firebase Authentication handles your email, password credentials and account identifier. Your display name, username and selected avatar, along with study records you choose to sync, are stored in Firebase Firestore under your account.
  • Optional AI requests: if you choose Gemini, relevant study prompts, material text, answers or tutor messages are sent through Firebase AI Logic to Google. If you connect ChatGPT and choose it, the relevant content goes to OpenAI. On-device generation runs on the phone after a model download. The app explains these choices before first use. Imported file bytes are processed locally; extracted text can be included in the AI request you select.
  • Optional community: joining the board shares your chosen username, weekly points and streak with signed-in learners. A small photo thumbnail is included only if you opt in. Other learners do not see your email or private studies. Users can report a public profile and hide it on their device.
  • Reports: an adult may report a public profile or generated question or tutor answer. The reason, content identifier, a short excerpt and submission time are sent to our private Firestore report queue for review. Reports are not visible to other learners.

Other services and device access

Firebase App Check with Play Integrity helps protect online services on adult routes. The optional Android speech recognition action may send audio to your phone's speech service; LetsStudy saves the returned transcript, not the audio. Optional notifications require device permission. The app has no advertising SDK and does not sell personal data. This support site is static and does not set tracking cookies.

Why we use data and who receives it

We use data to provide lessons, save progress, synchronize an optional account, deliver the AI provider you choose, operate the optional community board, review safety reports and respond to support requests. Google/Firebase and OpenAI receive only the information needed for the services you select, subject to their own terms and privacy notices. Your chosen online provider may process data outside your country.

Retention and control

Local data remains until you erase it in the app or remove the app's data. You can erase youth progress in the youth lessons. Adults can delete local and synced studies in Settings, leave the community board, remove a shared photo and delete their account and associated cloud study data in Settings. Account deletion removes the Firebase account, synced study records, profile and public board entry. Safety reports may need separate review; contact us to request deletion of a report linked to you. Data already sent to an AI or speech provider follows that provider's own retention terms.

You can also request deletion without the app. We may ask you to verify control of the email address before acting. Do not send us your password. We will explain any legally required retention when replying to a request.

Questions and updates

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, email admin@vallssolutions.com. We will update this page when the app or its data practices change.
