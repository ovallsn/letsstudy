Account and data control

Delete your LetsStudy account

You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled LetsStudy or cannot open the app.

Request deletion by email

Send a message from the email address used for your LetsStudy account with the subject “Delete my LetsStudy account.” We will reply to verify that you control the account and process the request. Never include your password or a one-time code.

Email deletion request

If you cannot access that email address, write to admin@vallssolutions.com and explain the situation so we can discuss another verification method.

Delete in the app

For adult accounts: open LetsStudy → Settings → Account & sync → Delete account and cloud data. Confirm with your password. This removes the account, synced studies and profile, and your community board entry. You can also delete studies separately in Settings.

Ages 9–17: the youth experience does not create an online account. Open the lesson list and choose “Erase my progress” to remove saved answers from this device.

What is removed

Account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, cloud study records, account profile and any public board entry. Local data on a device you still have can be erased in Settings or by clearing the app's storage. Safety reports and records held by an outside AI or speech provider may require separate handling; see the privacy policy. We will tell you if any data must be retained for a legal obligation.
