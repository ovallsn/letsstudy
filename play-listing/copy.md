# Proposed Google Play listing copy

**App name:** LetsStudy

**Short description:** Learn with age-appropriate lessons and focused practice.

**Full description:**

LetsStudy helps learners explore a topic, practise what they know and understand each answer.

For ages 9–12 and 13–17, LetsStudy offers curated offline lessons in maths, science and digital judgement. Progress stays on the device. Young learners do not need an account and cannot use the public community board or online AI tools.

Adults can create a personal study path from a topic, document, job description or language goal. Practise with questions, review explanations, ask a study tutor and save progress. Choose online Gemini, connect an eligible ChatGPT account, or download an optional on-device model. Online AI can make mistakes, so check important information against trusted sources.

An account is optional for adults who want to sync their study library across devices. The community leaderboard is also optional and shares only the profile details you choose. You can report content, block a profile, erase study data and request account deletion in Settings.

Privacy: https://letsstudy.vallssolutions.com/privacy/
Account deletion: https://letsstudy.vallssolutions.com/delete-account/
